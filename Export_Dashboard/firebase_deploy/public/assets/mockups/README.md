## PO Checklist Before Ordering

- Confirm final size/volume for each product against the actual menu.
- Confirm food-contact documents: declaration, TDS/MSDS, and test reports where applicable.
- Confirm artwork method: direct print, decal, UV DTF, embroidery, or blank stock.
- Confirm MOQ, lead time, sample fee, freight, VAT, and payment term.
- Replace every `estimate` price with a vendor quote before purchase order.
