# I Love BanhMi - Kraft Banh Mi Box Printer Specification

## 1. Box Purpose

Packaging box for approximately 10 banh mi sandwiches.

Each sandwich will already be wrapped in greaseproof paper, so the box does not need grease-resistant coating.

## 2. Recommended Finished Size

Usable inner size:

- Length: 360 mm
- Width: 260 mm
- Height: 120 mm

Approximate outer size:

- Length: 380 mm
- Width: 280 mm
- Height: 140 mm

The outer size may change slightly depending on carton thickness, locking tabs, lid structure, and handle cutouts.

## 3. Sandwich Layout

Recommended packing layout:

- 2 rows x 5 sandwiches
- Each sandwich assumed size: 220-240 mm long x 55-65 mm wide
- Leave 10-15 mm top clearance so sandwiches are not compressed

If the actual sandwich is longer than 240 mm, increase the inner box size to:

- 380 x 280 x 120 mm

## 4. Box Structure

Box type:

- Rectangular kraft carton food box
- Open-top presentation box with fold-over locking lid
- Side locking flaps
- Hand-carry cutouts on the top lid
- Front panel visible for branding
- Flat-packed dieline for assembly

Preferred structure:

- Auto-lock bottom or glued side seam, depending on supplier capability
- Secure front locking tab
- Rounded/softened handle cutout corners to avoid tearing

## 5. Material

Recommended material:

- Natural brown kraft carton board
- 350-400 gsm
- Matte kraft finish
- Food-contact suitable paper board

Coating:

- No greaseproof coating required
- No glossy lamination
- No plastic coating

Reason: every sandwich is individually wrapped in greaseproof paper before being placed into the box.

## 6. Printing Direction

Base material:

- Keep natural kraft brown as the main box surface

Print colors:

- Black
- White
- Brand red: #E31837
- Fresh sky accent: #7BDFF2
- Optional small green accent: #159C46

Brand placement:

- Main front panel: I Love BanhMi logo, large and centered
- Side panel: simple "BANH MI" text or brand mark
- Bottom/front edge: thin red and sky-blue stripe
- Optional light line-art illustrations: baguette, herbs, chili, cucumber

Printing style:

- Direct print on kraft paper
- Matte ink finish
- Avoid heavy full-surface ink coverage
- Keep most of the box as visible kraft texture

## 7. Estimated Panel Sizes For Dieline

These are working dimensions for quotation. Printer should adjust final dieline based on board thickness and locking mechanism.

Main panels:

- Front panel: 360 x 120 mm
- Back panel: 360 x 120 mm
- Left side panel: 260 x 120 mm
- Right side panel: 260 x 120 mm
- Bottom panel: 360 x 260 mm

Lid and flaps:

- Top lid panel: 360 x 260 mm
- Front locking flap: 360 x 35-45 mm
- Side tuck flaps: 260 x 35-45 mm
- Handle/carry cutouts: final size to be confirmed by printer

Bleed and safety:

- Bleed: 3 mm on all printed edges
- Safe margin: 5 mm from cut/fold lines
- Minimum line weight: 0.25 mm

## 8. Production Notes

Please confirm with the printer:

- Exact dieline and locking structure
- Maximum load strength for 10 wrapped sandwiches
- Whether 350 gsm is strong enough or 400 gsm is recommended
- Actual sample test with 10 real wrapped sandwiches before bulk order
- Color proof on kraft paper, because kraft will darken printed colors
