# I Love BanhMi Packaging Mockups

Ngay tao: 02/06/2026

Day la bo mockup concept de duyet huong nhan dien bao bi. Anh mockup khong phai artwork in, khuon be hoac thong so san xuat.

Khi san xuat, phai dung file vector goc `../../logo.ai` hoac `../../logo.svg`. Khong boc tach logo tu anh mockup.

Bang thong so va gia tien day du theo tung san pham nam tai `../README.md`.

## Huong nhan dien dang dung

- Nen Kraft nau tu nhien.
- Logo trang va den, diem nhan tim do va rau thom xanh.
- Pattern nho chi dung tren giay goi banh mi va bao ong hut.
- Cac bao bi con lai giu bo cuc toi gian, logo dat o mat chinh.

## Viec can chot truoc khi lam artwork in

- Kich thuoc va dung tich tung mau bao bi.
- Loai nap tuong thich voi tung ly va to.
- Vi tri logo, kich thuoc logo va so mat in.
- Co in truc tiep hay dung tem dan trong giai doan dau.
- Mau thuc te, ho so food-contact va thu nghiem chong tran/chong dau.
