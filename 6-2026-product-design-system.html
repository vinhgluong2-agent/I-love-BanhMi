I Love Banh Mi Product Design System 2026

Unified Product Line

Mint Green / Red / Black / Cream Packaging / Uniform / Signage

Packaging

Rule: mint is the brand field, cream is the food-safe paper base, black carries typography, red is used only as a sharp accent.

Greaseproof Wrapper

20 x 30 cm Sheet

Mint field, oversized logo block, small repeated typography. Use 1-color black/red simplified version for cost control.

Takeaway Bag

Kraft Square Bottom

Natural kraft stays visible. Front logo on cream patch, mint side band, red tamper seal option.

Carry Box

10 Banh Mi Box

Mint lid, kraft base, black type on side panels. Keep top face clean for delivery photos.

Cold Drink

PET Cup

Clear cup, front logo only. Mint/red lid seal or sleeve carries the stronger brand color.

PP Cup

Flat Bottom Cup

Semi-opaque cup with one-color black logo and red/mint sticker seal on top film.

Hot Drink

Paper Cup

Cream cup, mint vertical band, black wordmark. Red heart stays as the only hot accent.

Premium Hot Cup

2-Layer Silver Cup

Silver cup should be restrained: black logo, mint lid seal, no extra pattern on metallic surface.

Food Bowl

2-Tier Pho Bowl

Cream bowl sleeve with mint band. Put logo on the top tray seal where delivery photos see it.

Condiment

Sauce Cup

Keep cup blank if tiny. Use lid sticker: red heart + black wordmark on mint or cream.

Bottle

Clear PET Bottle

Minimal front label, black cap, mint neck seal. Avoid busy wraparound labels.

Delivery Seal

Sticker Set

Use red for warning/closure, mint for normal brand seals, cream for paper bag closure.

Straw

Paper-Wrapped Straw

Repeat black text on cream paper. Mint end marks help sorting by size.

Service Items

These need lower ink coverage and harder-wearing production choices. The design should be functional first.

To-Go Kit

Cutlery Sleeve

Mint sleeve, black type, red heart. Do not print on every utensil.

Paper Goods

Kraft Napkin

Small black corner logo. Mint/red only if vendor can hold registration cleanly.

Dine-In

Black Tray

Keep tray plain black. Brand through liner, not permanent tray print.

Tray
Liner

Dine-In

Tray Liner

This is the main dine-in storytelling surface: large black type, mint field, red accent strip.

Cup Lid

Film / Lid Seal

Use size-coded mint/red tabs. Logo must stay away from straw hole and heat seal edge.

Back Of House

Black Gloves

Glove itself stays black. Brand the box label only; keep hygiene info readable.

Uniform & Signage

Uniforms should be calmer than packaging. Signage can glow, but still follows mint as the 2026 primary field.

Uniform

Canvas Apron

Black/charcoal apron, chest logo patch, mint stitching/detail. Embroidery preferred.

Uniform

Black Polo

Left chest logo, no big back print. Add mint sleeve tab if needed.

Uniform

Cream Polo

Use black logo version if embroidery loses small white details.

Storefront

Neon Decor Sign

Approved direction: cream neon letters, red heart, mint backing glow, black wall.

Exterior

3D Blade Sign

Mint edge, black side wall, cream face. Keep shape simple for fabrication.

Storefront

Window Decals

Large sparse decals, not wallpaper. Preserve visibility into the shop.

Design Option 02

Youth Pop

More youthful and energetic: cobalt blue becomes the campaign field, lime adds motion, kraft keeps the food craft feeling, and the original logo stays unchanged.

Cobalt Street Energy

Use this option for launch drops, student areas, TikTok/Instagram content, staff tees, and limited packaging runs. It is louder than the mint system, so keep it as a campaign option instead of replacing the core brand.
