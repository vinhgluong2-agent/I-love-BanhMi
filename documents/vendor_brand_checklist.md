# VENDOR BRAND COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST
**Project:** I Love BanhMi (US Branch)
**Reference:** I Love BanhMi Brand Guideline 2026

*Dear Vendor/Fabricator,*
*Please carefully review and check off the following brand compliance requirements before proceeding with production. Any deviation from these rules may result in rejection of the final product.*

---

## 1. COLOR ACCURACY (Hệ Màu Tiêu Chuẩn)
- [ ] **Mustard Yellow:** Hex `#FFC107` (For Main Logo text/accents).
- [ ] **Vibrant Red:** Hex `#E31837` (For the Heart icon).
- [ ] **Pure White:** Hex `#FFFFFF` (For Window Decals and secondary elements).
- [ ] *All printed or illuminated materials must match these exact color codes under standard lighting conditions.*

## 2. LOGO CLEAR SPACE (Khoảng Lề An Toàn)
- [ ] **The "X" Rule:** The clear space surrounding the entire logo must be at least equal to "X", where "X" is the height of the Red Heart icon in the logo.
- [ ] No other graphical elements, text, or physical borders (like structural frames) are allowed inside this clear space.

## 3. LOGO MINIMUM SIZE (Kích Thước Tối Thiểu)
- [ ] For physical printing/production, the logo must never be smaller than **1.5 CM** in width.
- [ ] For digital screens (e.g., Digital Menu Boards), the logo must never be smaller than **40 PIXELS** in width.

## 4. STRICTLY PROHIBITED USAGES (Các Lỗi Cấm Tuyệt Đối)
- [ ] **NO Shape Alteration:** Do not stretch, squish, or distort the proportions of the logo.
- [ ] **NO Background Plates:** Do not place the logo inside a solid circular border or continuous background board (unless explicitly designed as a free-form 3D cut-out).
- [ ] **NO Color Swaps:** Do not change the color of the Red Heart or the text.
- [ ] **NO Font Substitution:** Do not re-type the logo using a different font. Always use the provided Vector source file.
- [ ] **NO Drop Shadows:** Do not add artificial drop shadows or outer glows to the logo file (physical halo-lit LED effects are allowed).

## 5. TYPOGRAPHY (Quy Chuẩn Phông Chữ Dành Cho In Ấn/Menu)
- [ ] Primary Headings & Menus: Must use **Montserrat Black** or **Montserrat Medium**.
- [ ] Secondary Signage (Approved exception): The specific string *"vietnamese food & coffee"* is allowed to use the approved Cursive/Script font as provided in the design file.
